I am currently a Ph.D. student at Sichuan University. I received my M.S. degree from Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology.

My research focuses on group decision making, quantum cognition, and human-AI collaboration. I am particularly interested in quantum decision analysis, preference learning, trust networks, grey system theory, and consensus models for uncertain decision environments.

I have authored or co-authored research articles in international journals including *IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics: Systems*, *IEEE Transactions on Fuzzy Systems*, *Information Sciences*, *Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence*, *Expert Systems with Applications*, *Advanced Engineering Informatics*, and *Computers & Industrial Engineering*.
